# Sunny Days — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 1 September 2026_

Sunny Days is a weather app for children. We have built it so that it collects
as little as possible about the people who use it — especially children.

## What we do not do

- We do **not** collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or accounts.
- We do **not** show advertising of any kind.
- We do **not** use third-party analytics, attribution or advertising SDKs.
- We do **not** sell or share personal information.
- We do **not** ask a child for payment details. Purchases happen only through
  Google Play, behind a grown-up gate.
- We do **not** collect persistent advertising identifiers.

## What the app uses, and why

**Approximate location (optional).** If you allow it, the app reads your
device's approximate location so it can show the weather where you are. The
coordinates are sent to our weather provider, Open-Meteo
(https://open-meteo.com), solely to fetch a forecast. Open-Meteo does not
require an account and we send nothing else with the request — no identifier,
no name, no device information. If you decline, the app shows a saved place
instead and works normally.

**Saved places.** The cities you save are stored on the device only.

**Play progress.** Stars, collected items, passport stamps and your explorer's
outfit are stored on the device only.

**Purchases.** If a grown-up buys a content pack, Google Play processes the
purchase. We receive from Google only whether a pack is owned. We never see
payment details.

**Reminders.** If enabled, the daily reminder is scheduled on the device. No
push token is requested and nothing is sent to a server.

## Children

Sunny Days is designed for children. It contains no ads, no external links out
of the app, no chat, no user-generated content, and no social features.

## Deleting your data

Everything the app stores lives on the device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
Purchases remain associated with your Google Play account and can be restored.
