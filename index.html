Sunny Days — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Sunny Days is a weather app for children. We have built it so that it collects as little as possible about the people who use it — especially children.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or accounts.
  • We do not show advertising of any kind.
  • We do not use attribution or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.
  • We do not sell anything in the app. There are no purchases.
  • We do not collect persistent advertising identifiers.
  • We do not request or use device location.

What the app uses, and why

No location access. The app does not ask for, and cannot read, your device's location. A grown-up chooses a town by name when the app is first opened. To fetch the forecast we send only that town's published coordinates to our weather provider, Open-Meteo (https://open-meteo.com) — nothing that identifies you, your device, or where you actually are.

Saved places. The places you save, and any names and icons you give them, are stored on the device only.

Weather Passport. The weather stamps a child has collected are stored on the device only.

Anonymous usage statistics. On Android, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics to count which screens and features are used (for example "the week was swiped" or "a place was added") so we can improve the app. These events contain no names, no free text, no town or location, and no advertising identifier: advertising-ID and Android-ID collection are switched off in the app's configuration, and the app blocks the advertising-ID permission entirely. Firebase assigns a random, app-specific installation identifier to group events from the same installation; it is reset when the app is uninstalled. Nothing is used for advertising or personalisation. A grown-up can turn this off at any time in Settings → Data → "Anonymous app usage", which stops collection immediately. Google's handling of this data is described on Firebase's privacy page: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Reminders. If enabled, the daily reminder is scheduled on the device. No push token is requested and nothing is sent to a server.

Children

Sunny Days is designed for children. It contains no ads, no external links out of the app, no chat, no user-generated content, and no social features.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores lives on the device. Uninstalling the app deletes it and resets the analytics installation identifier. To ask for any analytics data associated with an installation to be deleted, email the address below.
